MohAcademy began with a profound realization within the Cameroonian educational landscape: students possessed the intellect, but lacked the specialized tactical resources required to decode the GCE Advanced Level framework.

Our journey is fueled by reverse-engineering the exam process. From curating step-by-step mathematical proofs to configuring browser-based virtual chemistry laboratories, this platform was built line-by-line to ensure that no aspiring scientist is ever held back by a lack of clarity.

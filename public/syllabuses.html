A/L Science Examination Architecture

The Cameroon GCE Advanced Level science tracks demand rigid experimental dexterity, analytical calculation skills, and conceptual clarity across distinct assessment components.

Practical Component

Accounts for exactly 20% of the overall grade. Evaluates absolute laboratory mechanics, sample observation, quantitative titration, error analysis, and data logging procedures.

Theoretical Mastery

Accounts for 80% of total points divided between objective testing and structural long-form analytical responses designed to test rigorous proof formulation capabilities.

Physics A/L

Paper 1 (MCQ)

50 Questions | 1 hr 30 mins | 34% Weighting

Comprehensive objective tracking assessing knowledge, application, and structural reasoning across all curriculum phases.

Paper 2 (Theory)

Structured & Essay | 3 hrs | 46% Weighting

Section A features compulsory structured evaluation variables. Section B targets comprehensive analytical proof problems and derivations.

Paper 3 (Practical)

Laboratory Exam | 3 hrs 15 mins | 20% Weighting

Natively features experimental setups targeting mechanical oscillation properties, optical alignments, or electric circuit measurement calibrations.

Core Content Syllabus Domains
  • Unit 1: Mechanics & Materials (Kinematics, Dynamics, Hydrostatics, Gravitational Fields, Solid Elasticity parameters).
  • Unit 2: Waves and Optics (Simple Harmonic Motion, Wave Equations, Physical Wave Interference, Refraction, Wavefront Optics).
  • Unit 3: Thermal Physics (Kinetic Theory of Gases, Thermodynamics Principles, Thermal Energy Transfer Metrics).
  • Unit 4: Electricity, Magnetism & Fields (Coulomb's Law, DC Circuits, Capacitance, Electromagnetic Induction, Alternating Currents).
  • Unit 5: Atomic, Quantum & Electronics (Photoelectric Effect, Nuclear Decay, Radioactive Isotope Tracking, Operational Amplifiers).
715

Chemistry A/L

Paper 1 (MCQ)

50 Questions | 1 hr 30 mins | 34% Weighting

Rigid objective matrix testing fundamental reactions, conceptual naming conventions, and mathematical calculation routines.

Paper 2 (Theory)

Structured & Essay | 3 hrs | 46% Weighting

Evaluates Physical, Inorganic, and Organic paths. Requires structural mechanism sketches, balancing complex equations, and calculation steps.

Paper 3 (Practical)

Laboratory Exam | 3 hrs 15 mins | 20% Weighting

Rigorous volumetric redox/acid-base titrations, structural qualitative analysis tracking ion detection, and thermochemical logging.

Core Content Syllabus Domains
  • Unit 1: Physical Chemistry (Atomic Architecture, Chemical Bonding, Energetics/Enthalpy, Kinetics, Equilibrium States, Electrochemistry nodes).
  • Unit 2: Inorganic Chemistry (Periodicity across Periods 3 and 4, Group 2 Alkaline Earth Metals, Group 7 Halogens, d-Block Transition Metal Chemistry).
  • Unit 3: Organic Chemistry (Isomerism Dynamics, Alkanes/Alkenes/Arenes, Halogenoalkanes, Alcohols, Carbonyl Functional Tracks, Carboxylic Derivatives).
  • Unit 4: Synthesis & Spectroscopy (Multi-step structural reaction lines, Infrared Spectroscopy analysis, Mass Spectroscopy evaluation profiles).
710

Biology A/L

Paper 1 (MCQ)

50 Questions | 1 hr 30 mins | 34% Weighting

Core objective selection testing taxonomic biological properties, functional anatomical components, and ecological processes.

Paper 2 (Theory)

Structured & Essay | 3 hrs | 46% Weighting

Requires detailed physiological explanations, high-precision structural biological diagrams, and biochemical cycle flowcharts.

Paper 3 (Practical)

Laboratory Exam | 3 hrs 15 mins | 20% Weighting

Microscopic slide analysis, high-fidelity biological drawings of specimens, food test matrices, and ecological population logging drills.

Core Content Syllabus Domains
  • Unit 1: Cell Biology & Biochemistry (Organelle Architecture, Mitosis/Meiosis, Enzymes, Carbohydrates, Lipids, Protein Sequencing).
  • Unit 2: Energetics & Respiration (Autotrophic Photosynthesis Pathways, Light-dependent stages, Anaerobic & Aerobic ATP Respiration cycles).
  • Unit 3: Genetics, Evolution & Selection (Mendelian Inheritance Models, Nucleic Acid Replication, Mutations, Natural Selection, Speciation mechanics).
  • Unit 4: Form & Function of Organisms (Plant Transport, Mammalian Circulation, Nervous Coordination, Homeostasis, Endocrine Systems).
  • Unit 5: Ecology & Environmental Biology (Ecosystem Energy Flows, Nutrient Biogeochemical Cycles, Anthropogenic Environmental Impacts).
765

Mathematics A/L

Paper 1 (MCQ)

50 Questions | 1 hr 30 mins | 34% Weighting

Comprehensive objective questions covering pure applications, vectors, mechanics, and statistical data processes.

Paper 2 (Pure Mathematics)

Written Exam | 3 hrs | 46% Weighting

In-depth calculus proofs, complex number operations, matrix operations, sequences, series, and advanced vector analysis frameworks.

Paper 3 (Applied Modules)

Mechanics or Statistics | 3 hrs | 20% Weighting

Tracks chosen specialization paths. Focuses either on structural physics engine kinetics/statics or advanced probability distributions.

Core Content Syllabus Domains
  • Unit 1: Pure Math Foundation (Polynomials, Systems of Equations, Logarithmic Functions, Advanced Trigonometric Identities).
  • Unit 2: Coordinate Geometry & Vectors (Conic Sections, Parametric Vector Forms, Dot & Cross Product vector proofs).
  • Unit 3: Advanced Calculus (Differentiation by First Principles, Integration Techniques, Complex Differential Equations).
  • Unit 4: Applied Classical Mechanics (Projectiles, Friction Mechanics, Direct Collisions, Rigid Body Equilibrium Statics).
  • Unit 5: Probability & Statistics (Permutations, Combinations, Binomial & Normal Distributions, Hypothesis Assessment Testing).
